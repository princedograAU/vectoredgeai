About us

A consultancy that ships, not just advises

VectorEdge AI is a small Australian IT consultancy. We solve practical technology problems — including AI — and leave clients with systems they can actually run.

We have taken existing web apps that were stuck short of production, rebuilt them to a professional standard, and moved them onto current platforms and engineering practices. We have also designed and delivered new web applications for clients who needed something purpose-built.

AI is part of how we work, not a slogan. We use it where it reduces cost, risk, or time-to-value — and we leave it out where a simpler system is the better product.
